Gallery images — slide naming convention
========================================

Place your images here with names:
  slide-01.jpg
  slide-02.jpg
  slide-03.jpg
  ...
  slide-NN.jpg

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
Recommended: same aspect ratio for all slides (e.g. 16:9, ~1920x1080)

The carousel auto-detects files by numbering.
First image loads eagerly; rest lazy-load.
